ZORRITO MINERALS

# Creative Brief – Supplier Dossier Development

## Project Objective

Create a premium supplier dossier that presents Zorrito Minerals as a vertically integrated mining, processing and manufacturing company ready to supply international customers.

## Target Customers

Pet Valu, Pet Supplies Plus, Costco, PetSmart, Chewy, industrial distributors, engineering and energy companies.

## Visual Style

Modern, premium, clean, industrial, large photography, infographics, orange/black branding.

## Main Sections

Cover; Executive Summary; About Us; Mining Operations; Processing; Manufacturing; Product Portfolio; Industries; Quality; Export Capability; Private Label; Sustainability; Contact.

## Information Required

Company history, mine information, production capacity, products, packaging, logistics, export capability, TDS, SDS, COA, certifications.

## Images Required

Mine, processing plant, warehouse, packaging line, products, pallets, containers, laboratory, drone photography.

## Deliverables

Corporate Brochure, Product Catalog, Capability Statement, PowerPoint Presentation, Supplier Package, Print-ready PDF, Editable source files.

## Final Goal

Produce marketing material comparable to leading industrial mineral companies and suitable for presentations to major North American buyers.
